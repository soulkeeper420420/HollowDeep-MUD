**HollowDeep — confirmed live features | November 6, 2026**

The latest confirmed native installation completed successfully and retained existing characters, rooms, objects and Chronicle progress.

**Latest addition**
- `spells` (alias `spell`) lists your learned spells alphabetically, shows current/max mana and points to casting help. Use `help spell`. Normal casting requirements still apply.

**Also available in the installed release**
- `hstance balanced`, `hstance guarded` and `hstance aggressive`: saved physical-combat stances with different strengths and tradeoffs.
- `hclass progress`: check XP/gold requirements and training eligibility.
- `hquest next`: find your next exploration Chronicle goal. Chronicles save progress and award cosmetic badges.
- `hprofile` and `hprofile status`: view exploration achievements and a compact character summary.
- `hcommands mobile` and `hcommands social`: focused command guides for mobile and group play.
- Owner/Admin/Moderator roles through `hstaff`, with role hierarchy, announcements, timed mutes, kicks and staff audit records. Roles are attached to saved game characters.
- Native party play: `follow`, `group`, `gtalk`, `say` and `tell`.

**Connect:** `172.233.176.81`, port `4040`, Telnet, SSL off. Use `save`, then `quit` when leaving.

**Not live yet:** the builder/DM update stopped during testing. Assigned room ranges, Implementor, and the expanded timed XP/gold event limits are pending a successful repaired installation. Clans/guilds, remorts and additional classes remain design proposals. This announcement does not claim those features are deployed.
