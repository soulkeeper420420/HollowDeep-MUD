WARLOCK — PRIVATE CHARACTER-CREATION TEST

Role: Pact-bound spellcaster.
Prime attributes: Intelligence and Piety.
Starting stats: standard 65-point allocation (3–18 each).
Native training: Mage-compatible during this identity-only test.

This candidate tests the durability and isolation of Warlock identity.
The first Warlock combat skill, Pact Bolt, is NOT enabled in this build.
Pactbrand is planned as a rebirth-earned skill and is NOT available yet.

A Warlock must not receive Necromancer-only Raise Corpse abilities.
Conjure remains available to all classes.

This page describes a private Round 78E test build.
It does not indicate that Warlock is active on the live game.
